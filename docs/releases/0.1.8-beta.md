## Scope

This patch repairs settings geometry on iPad and desktop: selection colors now
follow Signal White's control accent, the iPad drawer is fully framed without a
bottom fade, and settings search, button, close, and section-heading treatment
is square and quiet. It also includes the pending continuous desktop and tablet
tab-strip baseline repair.
